ClipFleet - Privacy Policy

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

ClipFleet is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, ClipFleet accesses basic profile information (user.info.basic) and the permission to publish videos (video.publish) through TikTok's official API.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any third party. ClipFleet does not operate any server and does not collect personal data from other users.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time from the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions) and delete the local files.
